## Definition of ready for platform import

A product is ready only when its canonical SKU/EAN, owner, target channel, category,
required attributes, approved copy, media rights, price/tax class and redirect strategy
are confirmed. A swipe choice is a portfolio decision, not yet a complete PIM record.
